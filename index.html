About this site

Charlotte Marsh — UX designer, Bristol

Tom asked if I wanted to watch something together. I said yes and spent the next forty minutes on my phone. He didn't say anything. That was the moment.

I didn't go cold turkey — I tried that, lasted three days. Instead I started replacing specific habits with specific things. A Kindle instead of reading on my phone. An alarm clock instead of leaving it on the nightstand. A film camera instead of Instagram. Small swaps. The cumulative effect surprised me.

People kept asking what I'd switched to, so I made a list. Then the list got longer. This site is what I wish had existed when I started — honest about what works, honest about what doesn't, and not trying to sell you anything.

One rule: Everything here does one thing. The moment a device can check social media or open a browser, it comes off the list.
