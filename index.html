About Put It Down

This site exists because the problem isn't phones. It's that phones are designed to replace everything else — and most "digital wellness" content just points you to another app.

We believe the best solution is often a single-purpose device that does one thing well, without pulling you back into the feed. Every product here was chosen for that reason alone.

No affiliate links. No sponsored products. No commission. Just honest guidance from people who've tried to put their phones down — and found that real objects help.

Our rule: Every product listed here must do one thing well, without opening a door to distraction. The moment it can check social media, it comes off the list.
