Left out on purpose

Things people expect to find here, and why they aren't. The test: it does a job a phone does, it works without the phone after setup, and it doesn't open a door to anything else.

Plain watches, alarm clocks and landlines
They avoid the phone, but they don't do anything a phone does. That's a swap, not an alternative.
Headphones
Accessories, not replacements. Perfectly good ones exist, they're just not what this site is for.
Film cameras
Lovely things, but they give up what makes a phone camera useful (storing, sharing, finding a photo again) without replacing it.
Tablets that run any app
If it can install Instagram, it's a phone with a bigger screen.
Trackers you read in an app
Rings, bands and watches that only make sense on your phone's screen keep the phone in the loop all day.
Players and streamers you run from an app
WiiM, Sonos, Bluesound and friends use your phone as the remote, and screenless players like the Mighty 3 need Spotify on a phone to change what's on them. If you have to pick the phone up to choose a song, it doesn't count.
Kids' starter smartphones
Still a screen, just a cuter one.

Silent Mode is written in one editorial voice, and the voice is the site's. There's no single reviewer behind it, no invented person with a drawer full of gadgets, and nothing here claims anyone lived with these products for a year.

The idea is simple. A phone does a lot of jobs at once, and does most of them while trying to keep you there. For plenty of those jobs there's a device that does one job properly: a Kindle for reading, a music player for music, a camera for photos. The catch is knowing they exist.

Specs and prices come from manufacturers and retailers and change often, so check the product page before you buy. If something here is wrong or out of date, tell us.

One rule: Everything here does one job. If a device is built to let you check social media or browse the web, it comes off the list.
